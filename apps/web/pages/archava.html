A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Let's make
things clear.

What is Archava?+

Archava brings an AI assistant into your customer experience and your team's workflows. Start with chat, add voice or a human presence, and connect it to the business tools you need.

Can we keep our current website and CRM?+

Yes. Existing-site integration and connecting business systems are part of the proposed offering. The exact scope depends on your systems, their APIs, and what you want the assistant to do.

Do we need an avatar?+

Start with the presence your business needs. Chat works on its own. Voice adds spoken conversation, and Human adds a visible digital presence.

What can I try in this preview?+

You can explore the website, add and manage sample leads, review a sample AI follow-up, and explore CRM and operations screens. Data is fictional and changes stay in your browser. Live AI, voice, avatar, and CRM/ERP connections are later integration work.

Is the workspace the same for every industry?+

The foundation is shared. Labels, modules, workflows, and branding follow the client. The industry examples here illustrate the configuration approach.
